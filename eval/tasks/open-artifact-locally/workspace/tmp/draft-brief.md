# Brightmoor pilot, week 4 readout

Brightmoor Foods put Halyard in front of 210 staff across four sites on the
first of the month. This is where the pilot stands at the end of week four,
written for the steering call rather than for the ops channel.

## What landed

Rota publishing moved off the shared spreadsheet at all four sites in the first
nine days, which was two weeks ahead of the plan. Shift swaps are running
entirely in the app at Ridgeway and Colt Lane; the two smaller sites still run
about a third of their swaps through the group chat because the supervisors
there prefer it.

Overtime visibility is the part managers keep mentioning unprompted. Seeing the
cost of a shift while drafting it has changed how three of the four sites build
their weekends.

## What is stuck

Payroll export still needs a manual reconciliation pass every Friday because
Brightmoor books premiums against a cost centre that our export does not carry.
It is a one-field change on our side and a data request on theirs, and it has
been open for eleven days.

Two site managers have not logged in since week two. Both told us the same
thing: they were never shown the handover, they were only sent the invite.

## What we are asking for

A decision on whether the cost centre field ships in the current release or
waits for the next one. A named owner at Brightmoor for the two silent sites.
And agreement that week eight is the review point, not week six, because the
payroll cycle will not have turned over twice before then.
