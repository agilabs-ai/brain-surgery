We stopped reporting one number internally last quarter.

It was "installs". Not because it went down, but because it never told us anything
we could act on.

What replaced it was median time to recall, and the beta came in at
nine seconds to recall.

A person saves something and then, some days later, needs it back. Everything we
build sits between those two moments.

Installs measure the first moment only. Time to recall measures the second one.

The day we swapped them, three features on the roadmap stopped making sense and
we dropped them.

That is the whole case for picking your number carefully.
