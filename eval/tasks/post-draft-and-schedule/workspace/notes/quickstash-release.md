# Quickstash 1.0 — release notes

Quickstash is generally available today, published under the Apache-2.0 licence.

Install it with `npx toolshelf add quickstash`, or clone the source at
https://repo.example/toolshelf/quickstash — everything is public.

## What shipped

- Snippet capture from any shell, zero config
- Encrypted sync across machines, served from our CDN edge
- A CI/CD hook so a pipeline can stash its build artefacts
- Search that returns in under 40ms on a 10k snippet store

## Numbers from the closed beta

- 2,140 people on the beta over six weeks
- 61% stashed something within the first hour
- Median time from "where did I put that" to recall: 9 seconds
- 38% of them stashed something on a second machine in week one
