# Launch copy, final

We spent six months building a queue that empties itself, and the only thing
anyone asked us afterwards was why it took six months.

Here is what actually changed. Jobs used to sit until someone noticed them.
Now they do not sit. That is the whole product.

Three numbers from the first month of real traffic:

1. Median wait went from 41 minutes to 9 seconds.
2. Nobody has opened the admin console in three weeks.
3. Two people asked whether the queue was still running, because it looked
   empty. It was running. It was empty.

If you run a queue that somebody watches, we would like to take the watching
part away from you.
